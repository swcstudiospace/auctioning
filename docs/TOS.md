# Terms of service — auctioning.lol

This document is plain-English information, **not legal advice**. It describes
how auctioning.lol works and what you can expect from us. If you need advice
about your own situation, talk to a qualified Australian lawyer.

## The service

auctioning.lol is a community game. You earn or buy reputation points (RP) to
support projects and enter live races. Nothing here is an investment product, a
managed investment scheme, or a promise of returns.

## Free RP has zero cash value

- Free RP is promotional. It cannot be bought, sold, transferred, or cashed out.
- It lives entirely off-chain in our private ledger, expires weekly, and carries
  no redemption right of any kind.
- RP has zero cash value. It is not money, not a currency, and not redeemable
  for cash or any other consideration.
- It does not confer equity, revenue share, governance rights, or a stake in
  anything.

## Paid RP is consumable gameplay, not a token

- Paid RP buys gameplay utility: race entries, boosts, cosmetics. It is consumed
  when you use it.
- We publish paid purchases as receipts on Solana mainnet (`log_paid_rp`) for
  transparency. That receipt records spend provenance — it is not a token, a
  security, or a claim on future returns.
- There is no yield, no investment, and no secondary market for RP. None is
  provided by us, and we make no representation that RP has any resale value.

## Australia — Corporations Act and consumer law

- We designed the economy to sit outside the definition of a financial product
  under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth): no schemes, no managed investment
  products, no promises of returns.
- Free RP never touches a market; paid RP is consumed in-game. Neither is
  offered as, or intended to be, a financial product or advice.
- The Australian Consumer Law (ACL, Schedule 2 of the Competition and Consumer
  Act 2010) still applies to the service we supply. Consumer guarantees for
  faulty service are not excluded.

## Refunds (ACL)

If a paid-RP purchase fails and the ledger is not credited, that is faulty
service. Contact us via the community channels linked from the app within
**14 days** of that failed purchase. We will restore the service (credit the RP)
or refund the Whop charge — for faulty service only.

Race outcomes are not refundable. Losing a race, disliking a ranking, or
changing your mind after RP has been credited and consumed is not a fault in
the service.

## Disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of Australia. Raise a dispute via the
community channels first; we would rather fix it than argue about it. Serious
legal enquiries can request our formal contact address there.
